Music Player

Music on the SD card

Add music

Shown under the cover on the player. Use an existing name to add tracks to that album.
Any picture. It is cropped to a square and shrunk automatically.
    MP3 files are sent as they are. Other formats (FLAC, WAV, M4A, ...) are converted to MP3 here in the browser; this takes a while for long songs.

    Display

    255 is full brightness. The dimmed level takes effect on the screen as soon as you save and the timeout has passed.

    Sound

    Boost in dB, 0 = neutral. Changes are heard at once while music plays. Tuned for the 3FE22 speaker in a sealed enclosure of about 1.5 litres: very low notes are cut below 80 Hz to protect the speaker.

    Wi-Fi

    The new name and password are used the next time the Wi-Fi is switched on at the player; this connection keeps working until then. If you forget them, erase the settings (see the README) to get MyMusicPlayer / music1234 back.

    Battery

    Calibrate the battery voltage: measure the battery with a multimeter and enter it here V

    Firmware update

    Use the file ending in -ota.bin. The player restarts afterwards.

    Save screenshot
